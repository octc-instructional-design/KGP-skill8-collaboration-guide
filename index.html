Designing Collaboration That Works

A Faculty Guide to Essential Skill 8: Collaborate and Work in Teams

Start reading

The whole guide for print or PDF

“Graduates will collaborate with colleagues, become effective team members, and manage conflict.” This guide turns that sentence into something you can design, run, and grade, starting with the smallest version that honestly counts. It's practical guidance for face-to-face, hybrid, and online courses.
