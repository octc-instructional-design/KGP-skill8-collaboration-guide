Designing Collaboration That Works

A Faculty Guide to Essential Skill 8: Collaborate and Work in Teams

“Graduates will collaborate with colleagues, become effective team members, and manage conflict.” This guide turns that sentence into something you can design, run, and grade — starting with the smallest version that honestly counts. Practical guidance for face-to-face, hybrid, and online courses.
