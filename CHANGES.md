## Blackboard wording rewritten for the web

- **Removed Blackboard navigation phrases.** "Listed under this page," "listed alongside this one," "under 3.0/4.0," and "in this folder" are replaced with section numbers (for example, "3.1 through 3.4").
- **Replaced emoji item titles.** Titles like "1️⃣ QUICK Collaboration in Action" are replaced with section references ("4.1 | Quick Collaboration in Action").
- **Linked cross-references.** Cross-references were plain text in Blackboard. Every one is now a link.
- **Removed repeated opening sentence.** The sentence "Essential Skill 8: Collaborate and work in teams." no longer opens each page's summary. The chapter line above every title already names the skill.
- **Removed per-page items.** The print buttons and page footers are gone. Printing is handled by the print stylesheet and the full-guide print page.
- **Standardized titles.** Section titles now follow each page's own heading. Blackboard item names like "Conflict-Style Self-Check" and "Building Your Rubric" are gone.

## New text (not in the Blackboard version)

- **Home page.** Title, subtitle, and introduction are adapted from the cover of the Fall 2026 review booklet. Chapter descriptions are taken from the booklet's contents page.
- **Chapter overview pages for 7.0, 8.0, and 9.0.** Blackboard had folders here, not pages. Each overview has a one-paragraph introduction drawn from the pages it introduces.
- **10.0 From Your Colleagues.** The page now lists six colleague projects as PDF packets (see the October 29 entry below). It also links to the CPE resource repository.

## Accessibility and structure

- **Footer text contrast.** The original footer used `#999` on white (2.8:1). That text now uses the site's muted color (6.5:1).
- **Other contrast fixes.** The reach table's "No" cells, the column subheads, and the gold "tag" text all pass WCAG AA now.
- **Decision lists.** The six decisions in 4.4 and 5.0 are real ordered lists. Each has a spoken "Part N" or "Decision N" label and a link target, so references like "Level 4 · Sustained, Part 2" go straight to it.
- **Tables on phones.** Tables scroll inside a labeled, keyboard-focusable box instead of widening the page.
- **Table headers.** Empty corner header cells have a screen-reader label.
- **Section order.** Blackboard listed 4.0 before 3.0. The site uses numeric order.

## Design (October 28, 2026 revision)

- **Pathfinder layout.** The layout now follows the FYE 100 Pathfinder Guidebook:
  - a running header with the guide title
  - a chapter sidebar listing the chapter's pages and the sections of the current page
  - breadcrumbs and a "Section N.N" kicker
  - rest-stop dividers between major sections
  - previous/next pills at the bottom of each page
- **Callouts.** The solid gold callout boxes are now soft gold notes. The gold "Builds 8.x" chips are sky-blue capsules.
- **No gold text on navy.** Number circles are a soft blue disc with a blue numeral. Labels on navy are white. The home page's eyebrow line uses a light sky tint.
- **Light panels.** The "For Faculty" and "You Have Backup" panels are light blue instead of navy.
- **Fewer lines:**
  - Section headings use the Pathfinder highlight style instead of a gold underline.
  - The gold band under each title is a single line of small text.
  - Boxes no longer carry side or top accent bars. The one exception is the activity cards on the level pages, where the bar's shade still marks the level.
  - List items have no divider lines.
  - Tables use horizontal rules only.

## Color, spacing, and site links (October 28, 2026, second revision)

- **Chapter banners.** Every chapter page (1.0–10.0) opens with a navy banner showing a sky-blue chapter number and the Skill 8 coin.
- **Level colors.** The four levels use one scale from the KCTCS palette, getting darker as the level gets bigger: PMS 298 sky (Quick), PMS 300 (Structured), KCTCS Blue (Coordinated), and PMS 282 navy (Sustained). The scale appears in five places:
  - the 4.0 chart and level cards
  - the 1.0 range
  - the 3.0 reach table
  - the activity cards and idea banks
  - a four-step meter under the title of each level page and both exemplars
- **Teaching-format colors.** Each format has its own color: gold for in person, sky for online, and PMS 300 for hybrid. The colors appear on the indicator cards (3.1–3.4), in the 2.0 tables, and in the setting rows on 4.1–4.3.
- **Even spacing.**
  - Blocks within a part of a page are 36px apart.
  - A rest stop marks each new titled section.
  - The template pages (7.1–7.4) have no rest stops and read as one form.
  - Fixed the spots where tables, notes, and boxes touched the content next to them.
- **Links back to the main site.** "All 10 Skills" in the header now opens the main Essential Skills site in the same tab, as the parent of this guide. Breadcrumbs now run All 10 Skills › Skill 8 Guide › Chapter › Section.
- **Capsules.** The "Builds 8.x" capsules on the activity cards and the indicator capsules on the Group Contract now sit in the top-right corner of their boxes.
- **8.1 Blackboard help.** The link now goes straight to Blackboard's Groups help page, with direct links to Create and Manage Groups, Group Assignments, and Group Discussions. Before, it went to the general instructor help home.

## Colleague project packets (October 29, 2026)

The six projects in the Blackboard org's colleague area are now PDF packets in `files/colleagues/`, listed on 10.0. They were contributed by Sherri Basil and adapted with her permission.

**How they're built**
- Each packet opens with a cover page: summary, course, length, team size, format, final product, and the closest level on the guide's scale. The level is an editorial match to 4.0. It wasn't part of the original materials.
- Faculty notes come first. Each student-facing section starts on a new page so it can be printed or copied into Blackboard on its own. A "For faculty" or "For students" capsule marks every section.
- The PDFs are tagged:
  - real headings and lists
  - table header cells
  - a bookmark outline
  - document title and language
  - logo alt text, with the decorative coin marked as an artifact

  The source pages pass axe-core with no violations.

**Edits made in every packet**
- Typos fixed: "Ceremonial," "Preparation," "preferred," "etc.," "choose," "set up."
- The skill name is standardized to "Essential Skill 8: Collaborate and Work in Teams." It had appeared as "KCTCS Essential Skill #8" and "Collaboration & Teamwork."
- Links to the contributor's OneDrive and SharePoint files were removed, because readers outside her account can't open them. Two of these were "Writing Performance-Based Assessments" and "Essential Skill 8 Assessment & LOMs." The third was the "Asynchronous Group Project Design Template," which is now named in the text without a link.
- Blackboard dividers, the empty headings, and the emoji check marks were removed.
