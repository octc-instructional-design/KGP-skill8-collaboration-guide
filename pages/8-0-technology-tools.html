8.0 Technology Tools

Two workspaces and the boundary between them — what to use, what order, what bites.

Two Workspaces

Blackboard is the official record. Teams is the workspace. Planning, chat, and drafting can happen in Teams; every graded deliverable submits in Blackboard. These pages cover the decisions, order of operations, and settings that matter for group work — not click-by-click steps.

  • 8.1 Using Blackboard for Group Work Not a how-to-click-Blackboard guide — the group-work decisions, dependencies, and boundaries that the step-by-step articles won't make for you.
  • 8.2 Using Microsoft Teams for Group Work Not a how-to-click-Teams guide — the group-work decisions and boundaries for using Teams as a team's workspace.
