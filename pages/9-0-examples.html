9.0 Examples

Three idea banks sorted by field, plus two fully worked Sustained projects.

What's Here

The idea banks are brainstorms sorted by field, sized to one level each and meant to be borrowed. The two Sustained exemplars are fully worked projects you can adapt.

  • 9.1 Quick Collaboration: Idea Bank Short, low-stakes moves that build Indicator 8.1 — contributing ideas toward a shared goal. Sorted by field, meant to be borrowed.
  • 9.2 Structured Collaboration: Idea Bank Short exchanges with a little structure that build Indicator 8.2 — communicating clearly, giving and receiving feedback, handling disagreement. Sorted by field, meant to be borrowed.
  • 9.3 Coordinated Collaboration: Idea Bank Short tasks with genuinely divided labor that build Indicator 8.3 — breaking a goal into parts and distributing roles by strength.
  • 9.4 Geologic Site Investigation (GLY 111) A fully online GLY 111 team project where each member analyzes a different line of geologic evidence and the team must reconcile conflicting observations into one interpretation.
  • 9.5 Interpersonal Communication in Action (COM 252) A fully online COM 252 project where a team builds a shared resource together — and then turns the lens on itself, analyzing its own communication with the concepts the course teaches.
