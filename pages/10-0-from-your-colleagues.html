Shared projects

These Skill 8 projects come from KCTCS faculty courses. Each packet has notes for faculty and, on separate pages, student-facing instructions you can copy into Blackboard. Packets are listed from the smallest level to the largest. The level shown is the closest match in this guide's scale (4.0).

Contributed by Sherri Basil and adapted with permission.

  • PDF

    Communication · Speech or Writing Feedback

    Peer Review Workshop

    Students review each other's draft speeches, presentations, or papers using a “3 strengths + 3 suggestions” structure, then revise their own work. A short, repeatable activity for practicing constructive feedback.

    Level 2 · Structured·Sections for faculty

    Open the packet: Peer Review Workshop (PDF, 2 pages, 154 KB)

  • PDF

    Communication · Persuasive Speaking

    Persuasive Speech Planning Team Challenge

    Four short group activities that take students from persuasion basics to a speech plan: a review discussion, a fact/value/policy classification challenge, an organizational-pattern match-up, and an audience analysis discussion. Use them in sequence or one at a time.

    Level 2 · Structured·Sections for faculty and students

    Open the packet: Persuasive Speech Planning Team Challenge (PDF, 14 pages, 360 KB)

  • PDF

    Geology · Relative and Absolute Dating

    Earth's Timeline Challenge

    Small groups work asynchronously to solve a geologic mystery. They read rock layers, fossils, and radiometric data, build a timeline of events, and present their conclusions in a shared presentation or infographic.

    Level 3 · Coordinated·Sections for faculty and students

    Open the packet: Earth's Timeline Challenge (PDF, 6 pages, 203 KB)

  • PDF

    GLY 110 · Environmental Geology

    Valley Creek Environmental Hazard Role-Play

    Students take the roles of community stakeholders, from the county emergency manager to a local business owner, after flooding, landslide risk, and possible groundwater contamination hit a fictional Kentucky town. Each team negotiates a shared set of recommendations for county leaders.

    Level 3 · Coordinated·Sections for faculty and students

    Open the packet: Valley Creek Environmental Hazard Role-Play (PDF, 7 pages, 219 KB)

  • PDF

    COM 181 · Basic Public Speaking

    Special Occasion Presentation Team Project

    Teams of three to five plan, write, rehearse, and deliver a ceremonial presentation, such as a retirement tribute or an awards ceremony, as one event-speaking team. The project runs six weeks and is built with backward design. It pairs COM 181 SLOs 2 and 5 with the Milestone behaviors of Essential Skill 8.

    Level 4 · Sustained·Sections for faculty and students

    Open the packet: Special Occasion Presentation Team Project (PDF, 14 pages, 331 KB)

  • PDF

    Communication · Persuasive Public Speaking

    Community Advocacy Campaign

    Teams pick a community issue, research it from several sides, and deliver an 8–10 minute public awareness presentation with a call to action. The six-week project pairs communication SLOs 2 and 3 with Essential Skill 8.

    Level 4 · Sustained·Sections for faculty and students

    Open the packet: Community Advocacy Campaign (PDF, 11 pages, 303 KB)
