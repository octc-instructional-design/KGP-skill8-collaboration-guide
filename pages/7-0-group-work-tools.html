7.0 Group Work Tools

Four ready-to-use templates, in the order you'd use them across a term.

The Templates

Use them in this order across a term: the survey and self-check in Week 1, the contract when teams form, and the peer evaluation at the midpoint and the end. For Quick and Structured activities, skip the survey and the contract — a two-day task doesn't need them.

  • 7.1 Collaboration Profile Survey A Week 1 intake that helps you form workable teams — and gives students an early prompt to think about how they work.
  • 7.2 How I Handle Disagreement: A Self-Check A short Week 1 self-check — six questions, no score, no result. You'll come back to it at the end of the project.
  • 7.3 Group Contract Your team's agreement about how you'll work together.
  • 7.4 Peer Evaluation A confidential rating of each teammate's collaboration, built on the four indicators.
